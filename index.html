Racing Line App

Draw a track on flat ground or real-world terrain and the solver returns the minimum-lap-time racing line. Then orbit it, or drive the optimal lap from a driver's-eye camera in 3D. Two solvers sit behind it: a fast curvature heuristic, and a periodic optimal-control problem over 360 collocation nodes solved with IPOPT through CasADi. 15K+ lines and 590+ tests later it also trains its own drivers in the browser, evolving neural-net policies with CMA-ES across parallel Web Workers.

FastAPI · Gemini 2.5 Flash · 2026

CareRouter

An AI mental-health triage system that gets people to the right support fast. I was the sole backend developer on a four-person team: a rule-constrained Gemini pass scores severity, urgency and issue type from a six-question intake, a second pass filters real facilities on cost, transport and language, and crisis cases route deterministically to 9-1-1 or 9-8-8 without ever touching the model. Safety-first by construction, not by policy.
